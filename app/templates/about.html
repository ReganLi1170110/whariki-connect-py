About Whāriki Connect

One platform for the work that happens around every child.

Whāriki Connect is a web platform for New Zealand early childhood education services. It brings attendance, learning story documentation, parent–teacher communication and accident reporting together in one place, so the information teachers already record becomes something everyone can actually use.

Why we built it

Licensed ECE services in Aotearoa follow Te Whāriki, the national early childhood curriculum, and keep daily records covering attendance, learning documentation, and health and safety. A range of digital platforms already support parts of this work, but none of them cover the whole of a teacher's day. In practice, kaiako end up moving between separate systems — and sometimes paper — to complete routine administration, with limited support for making sense of the information once it has been entered.

Whāriki Connect takes a different starting point: rather than one tool per task, it treats attendance, documentation, communication and incident reporting as parts of the same relationship with the same child. The name comes from Te Whāriki itself, where whāriki means "woven mat" — different strands brought together into one whole.

📖

Learning stories

Write observations, attach a photo, and tag them against all five Te Whāriki strands and all twenty learning outcomes. Save a draft while you finish it, then publish when it's ready for whānau to read.

🗓️

Attendance

Whānau check their child in at drop-off and out at pick-up. Teachers get a month calendar and can record or correct any day, including back-dated absences and sick days.

🩹

Accident & incident forms

A digital version of the accident report teachers already use, covering the incident, first aid given, and how and when whānau were contacted. Parents review and acknowledge it in the app.

💬

Messages

Direct messages between kaiako and whānau, kept alongside the child they're about, instead of scattered across email and social media groups.

If you're a parent or caregiver

Once your centre's administrator has approved your account, here's how to get started.

  1. Create an account. Choose Sign up, select Parent, pick your centre from the list, and submit. Your request goes to the centre administrator, who links your account to your child before approving it.
  2. Check your child in and out. Open Attendance when you arrive and tap Check in; tap Check out when you collect them. Open the calendar on any child to see their attendance history.
  3. Read learning stories. Learning stories shows every published story written about your tamariki, with the strands and learning outcomes each one relates to. Filter by child if you have more than one enrolled.
  4. Review accident forms. If an incident is recorded, you'll see it under Accident forms and get a notification. Open the report and tap Acknowledge this form so your centre knows you've seen it.
  5. Message your child's kaiako. Use Messages to reach the teachers in your child's room. A red dot next to a name means there's something new for you to read.

If you're a teacher

Kaiako accounts can document learning, manage attendance and record incidents.

  1. Create an account. Choose Sign up, select Teacher, then pick your centre and the room you work in. Your centre administrator approves the request.
  2. Write a learning story. Go to Learning stories → New learning story. Choose the child, write your observation, add a photo if you have one, and tick the strands and learning outcomes it relates to. Save draft keeps it private to you; Publish story makes it visible to that child's whānau and notifies them.
  3. Manage attendance. Attendance shows today's status for every child in your room. Open a child's Calendar and click any day to set it to present, absent, sick or on leave, and to adjust check-in and check-out times — including for days that have passed.
  4. Record an accident. Accident forms → Record accident opens a digital version of the paper report: date and time, location, nature of injury, what happened, first aid provided, and the parent-contact details. Save draft to finish later, or Submit form to send it to the child's whānau for acknowledgement.
  5. Keep in touch. Messages lists the parents of children in your room, so you can follow up on anything from the day.

If you're a centre administrator

  1. Approve new accounts. When someone signs up, you'll see a badge on Pending requests. Review each one and approve or decline it. When approving a parent, tick which child or children to link them to — this is what gives them access to the right learning stories, attendance and accident forms.
  2. Watch the centre dashboard. The Admin panel shows enrolments, staff and whānau numbers, attendance for the day, learning stories posted, and any accident forms still waiting on parent acknowledgement.
  3. Keep the directory current. The staff and families table shows every account and whether it is approved, pending or declined.

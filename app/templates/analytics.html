🍽️ Routine trends by child ▸

Weekly meal score and toileting issue rate for one child — pick a child below to see whether things are trending better or worse over time.

Meals eaten (score): each meal is scored by how much the child ate — eating all of it scores 100, most of it scores around 66, some of it scores around 33, and none of it scores 0. The chart shows the average of those scores across every meal recorded that week, so 100 means they consistently finished their food that week, and 0 means they were consistently eating little to nothing.
Toileting issues (%): out of every nappy change or toilet visit recorded that week, this is the share that needed extra attention — a soiled nappy or a toileting accident, rather than a routine wet nappy or a successful toilet trip. 0% means no issues that week; 30% means roughly 3 in every 10 toileting events needed extra care.

About us
Early Childhood Education

Everything about your tamariki, woven into one place.

Whāriki Connect brings learning stories, daily attendance, accident and incident forms, and parent–teacher messages into a single platform — so teachers stop moving between three or four separate systems just to get through the day.

  • Learning stories tagged to all five Te Whāriki strands and all twenty learning outcomes, with photos.
  • Attendance whānau can record themselves at drop-off and pick-up, with a calendar teachers can correct.
  • Accident forms that follow the paper report teachers already know, sent straight to the right whānau.
  • Messages between kaiako and whānau, kept alongside the child they're about.

Built for New Zealand ECE services following Te Whāriki, the national early childhood curriculum.
